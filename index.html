Architecture

An in-process autopilot runs the full loop every five minutes. Data flows left-to-right into a gated execution path; every fill is journaled, a weekly AI retrospective distills learnings, and those learnings feed back into the AI overlay — a closed, auditable learn→decide cycle.

What it does

Multi-factor scoring
Value, momentum, quality, sentiment, and open-market insider buying — winsorized-z, sector-neutral. Dead factors auto-drop and weights renormalize, so an empty feed never dilutes the blend.
LLM advisory overlay
Claude reviews the quant shortlist as a panel of investor lenses → buy / hold / avoid + conviction, then conviction-weights sizing. Fail-open (any error → pure quant); content-hashed to ~1 call/day.
Self-improving retro loop
A weekly AI retrospective grades matured trades vs SPY, writes curated learnings, and feeds them back into the overlay's prompt — a closed learn → decide loop with full provenance.
Layered, fail-closed risk
Daily circuit breaker on realized P&L, a dated kill switch, per-trade guard + position caps, and an atomic single-instance lock. Every default is “do not trade.”
Backtest promotion gate
Walk-forward validation against pre-registered out-of-sample bars (≥8 OOS windows, Sharpe / hit-rate thresholds) gates any strategy from paper toward capital — set before tuning.
Resilience & audit
Broker-truth reconcile every tick, append-only fsync'd decision journal (scores + AI verdict + prompt-version hash), a hidden self-healing watchdog + external dead-man ping, and staged rollout.

Ambient monitor & stack

A read-only Raspberry-Pi kiosk overlays a live equity/P&L HUD on an ambient weather display — token-gated, self-supervised, and launched hidden at boot.
